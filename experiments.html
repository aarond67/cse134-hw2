MathML: mass-energy equivalence

Einstein’s mass-energy equivalence equation is E=mc2 . Here, E is energy, m is mass, and c is the speed of light in a vacuum.

MathML was part of the HTML specification before all major browsers supported it. That meant a formula could work in one browser and fail in another. Firefox and Safari supported it earlier, while Chrome added MathML Core in version 109. That gap made it harder to rely on MathML for a public website.

Source: Can I use: MathML browser support.
